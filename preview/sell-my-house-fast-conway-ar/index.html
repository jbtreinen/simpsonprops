Conway questions

How fast can I sell my house in Conway?

We can usually make a written cash offer within 24 hours of seeing the house and close in about 30 days, depending on the title company. You can also pick a later date.

Do you buy houses in Conway that need repairs?

Yes. We buy Conway houses as-is, including ones with foundation, roof, plumbing, or other major problems. You do not need to fix or clean anything.

Does it cost anything to sell my Conway house to you?

No. There are no commissions or fees to us, and we pay the normal closing costs. Any mortgage or liens are paid off at closing.
