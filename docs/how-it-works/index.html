How selling to us works

Selling your house for cash takes three steps and usually about two weeks. You never pay commissions, and you do not fix anything.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us about the house

    Fill out the short form or call. Takes about two minutes.

  2. 2

    Get a fair cash offer

    We look at the house once and give you a written offer, usually within 24 hours.

  3. 3

    Close when you want

    A local title company handles the paperwork. You get paid at closing.

Selling to us vs. listing with an agent

Selling to Simpson PropertiesListing with an agent
CommissionsNoneOften 5 to 6%

Listing can get a higher price for a house in great shape. A cash sale trades some price for speed, certainty, and no repairs. We will help you figure out which fits you.
